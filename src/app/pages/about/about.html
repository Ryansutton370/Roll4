About

Roll4 is mostly for me and my circle of friends whose preferred playstyle is usually not represented in the digital tools currently available. I'm hoping it is comprehensive, intuitive, and minimalist enough to feel "right" for you the way it feels for me. I intend to use it mostly in-person, but those using VTT might find it a useful supplemental tool in some way.

Roll4 was also an excuse to play around with Angular development. I am a software engineer by day in the functional automation testing sphere, I'd like to start branching out. I intend to continue expanding Roll4 for a while both in terms of gameplay functionality and improving its technical design.

How it's made

Roll4 runs entirely in your browser as a single-page app — nothing to install. Signing in creates a free account backed by Firebase, Google's app-hosting platform, which stores your Players, NPCs, Parties, Troops, and Encounters and keeps them available across devices.

Under the hood it's built with Angular, a widely used framework for building web app interfaces, structured as a set of pages (Players, NPCs, Encounters, Generators, and so on) backed by small services that own each type of data. The name tables, monster stat blocks, spell lists, and other reference content used by the Generators pages are drawn from openly licensed tabletop rulesets (credited on each relevant page) and hand-curated for this site.
