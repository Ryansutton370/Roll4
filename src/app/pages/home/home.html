About

There are plenty of good initiative trackers out there already. None of them quite worked the way I actually run combat at the table, though, so Roll4 started as an attempt to recreate what I was already doing in my notebook.

Roll4 is primarily meant to facilitate Chaotic Initiative: every combatant re-rolls initiative at the start of each round instead of locking in one order for the whole fight. Roll4 also supports standard "vanilla" initiative, but I hope you give Chaotic a try. With Chaotic Initiative, nobody knows when their turn is coming next, so players stay locked in instead of tuning out until their name comes back around.

Ties are settled first by whoever has the highest initiative modifier, and then by a dice roll after that.
